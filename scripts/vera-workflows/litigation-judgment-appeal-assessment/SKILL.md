# Judgment and Appeal Assessment

Work only from the current Matter's pinned DocumentVersions, including the judgment or order under assessment and any verified authority already imported into the Matter. This workflow supports civil and commercial first-instance judgment assessment, appeal, and retrial. Respect the procedural stage and represented side confirmed by counsel. Do not infer an appeal deadline, standard of review, ground, or procedural consequence that is not supported by a pinned source or verified authority.

Create one Matter-owned, cited editable Word document titled `Judgment and appeal assessment`. It is the lawyer's working assessment, not an appeal filing or an external communication. It must distinguish: the decision and its exact record locator; the represented side and procedural posture; the material holdings and reasoning actually stated in the decision; source-supported potential grounds, responses, or retrial issues appropriate to the confirmed stage and side; legal authority and pinpoint locators where available; record support and conflicts; unresolved facts or law; and the next lawyer action. Keep the court record and legal authority visibly distinct.

Process: read the pinned case record and authority sources; identify the stated decision, reasoning, and procedural posture; assess only source-supported grounds or responses; create the cited editable assessment; verify the current work product, source pins, and every material citation. Pause on a missing judgment or order, source-version drift, material citation gap, missing authority needed for a proposed legal ground, unresolved deadline or standard-of-review question, evidence conflict, missing fact, required lawyer decision, material scope expansion, or consequential action. Drafts are not lawyer approval or external delivery; final and external use remain subject to the existing Review and export gates.
